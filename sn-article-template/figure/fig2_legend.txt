Figure 2 | Technical architecture of the knowledge-guided masked attention mechanism and hierarchical graph representation.

a, Knowledge-guided masked attention mechanism. Node feature matrix H ∈ ℝ^(N×d) is linearly projected into query (Q), key (K), and value (V) representations via learnable weight matrices W_Q, W_K, and W_V, respectively. The raw attention scores are computed as QK^T and combined with a knowledge mask M derived from the Reactome/PPI knowledge base, where M_ij = −∞ if no curated biological relationship exists between nodes i and j, effectively zeroing out attention weights for biologically unsupported interactions after softmax normalization. The final attention matrix A = Softmax(QK^T/√d + M) is multiplied with V to produce updated node representations H'. This mechanism encourages information propagation to follow curated biology.

b, Hierarchical graph definition. The tissue microenvironment is modeled as a three-level hierarchical graph. Gene Layer (L0) captures regulatory relationships between genes based on established gene regulatory networks. Cell Layer (L1) represents cellular neighborhoods where nodes are individual cells connected by physical proximity (dashed lines) or functional interactions (solid arrows). Tissue Layer (L2) encodes higher-order tissue motifs (T1, T2) representing recurring spatial patterns of cell communities. Cross-layer edges (dashed vertical lines) link genes to their expressing cells and cells to their containing tissue motifs, enabling multi-scale message passing.

c, In silico perturbation analysis. Given an original graph G, a perturbed graph G' is constructed by removing a target edge (indicated by red X). Both graphs are passed through the trained model f(·) to obtain risk predictions. The perturbation sensitivity ΔRisk = f(G) − f(G') measures how blocking an interaction changes predicted risk (consistent with Fig. 1d). Positive ΔRisk indicates the blocked interaction increases predicted risk (therapeutic hypothesis), while negative ΔRisk suggests the interaction is protective. Note: In the figure, Risk_1 corresponds to f(G') and Risk_2 corresponds to f(G), thus Δ as displayed equals −ΔRisk by our convention.

Abbreviations: PPI, protein-protein interaction; Q, query; K, key; V, value.
