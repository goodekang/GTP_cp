Supplementary Figure S2 | Hyperparameter optimization and architecture search.

a, Feature backbone comparison. Forest plot comparing downstream survival prediction performance (C-index, mean ± 95% confidence interval) across ten feature extraction backbones, evaluated on BRCA validation set with fixed downstream architecture. Models are ordered by performance and annotated with parameter counts. 

ViT-H/14 pretrained with CONCH (632M parameters) achieves the highest C-index of 0.740 (diamond marker), followed by ViT-L/14 with UNI pretraining (304M, 0.717) and scGPT for genomic features (51M, 0.703). Foundation models pretrained on histopathology data (CONCH, UNI) substantially outperform general-purpose ImageNet-pretrained backbones such as ResNet-50 (25M, 0.665), ResNet-18 (11M, 0.638), and Inception-v3 (24M, 0.633). 

Based on these results, ViT-H/14 (CONCH) was selected as the image encoder for all subsequent experiments (Fig. 1a). Domain-specific genomic encoders (scGPT, Geneformer) achieve competitive performance despite smaller model sizes, highlighting the value of domain-appropriate pretraining for multimodal integration. Note: C-index values here are from single-dataset ablation and differ from pan-cancer benchmarks in Fig. 3.

b, Transformer architecture search. Heatmap showing C-index performance across a grid of transformer configurations varying the number of attention heads (1, 2, 4, 8, 16; x-axis) and transformer layers (2, 4, 6, 8, 12; y-axis). 

The optimal configuration (6 layers × 4 heads, C-index = 0.733 on BRCA validation set, highlighted with brackets) balances model capacity and regularization. Shallow architectures (2 layers) underperform regardless of head count, while very deep models (12 layers) show diminishing returns and potential overfitting. Intermediate depths (4–8 layers) with moderate head counts (4–8) achieve robust performance across the grid.

c, Masked tissue modeling (MTM) masking ratio optimization. Dual-axis plot examining the effect of masking ratio during self-supervised pretraining on downstream performance (C-index, dark blue circles, left axis) and pretraining reconstruction loss (orange squares, right axis).

Performance peaks at 30% masking ratio (labeled "Best: 30%", red diamond), achieving C-index = 0.74 on BRCA validation set. The teal shaded region (30–50%) indicates the optimal operating range where the pretraining task is challenging enough to learn meaningful representations without degrading downstream transferability. 

Lower masking ratios (10–20%) result in trivial reconstruction tasks with suboptimal representation learning. Higher ratios (>60%) increase reconstruction difficulty (rising loss curve) but harm downstream performance as the model struggles to recover heavily corrupted inputs, confirming findings from masked autoencoder literature.

Abbreviations: C-index, concordance index; CI, confidence interval; ViT, Vision Transformer; CONCH, Contrastive learning from Captions for Histopathology; UNI, Universal Image encoder; CLIP, Contrastive Language-Image Pretraining; MTM, masked tissue modeling.
