Supplementary Figure S4 | Spatial mechanism supplements and cell-cell interaction analysis.

a, Spatial cell-cell interactions associated with survival. Table and schematic showing the top-ranked protective spatial motifs identified by the model. Each row displays a cell-cell interaction motif (visual icon), interaction name, enrichment z-score, and hazard ratio (HR).

Identified protective interactions:
- CD8T–Tumor (z = −8.3, HR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.18–0.42): Direct contact between CD8+ T cells and tumor cells, indicating active cytotoxic immune engagement.
- Macrophages–Tumor (z = −8.0, HR = 0.31, 95% CI: 0.21–0.46): Macrophage infiltration into tumor regions, suggesting M1-polarized anti-tumor activity.
- DC–Tumor (z = −6.6, HR = 0.35, 95% CI: 0.23–0.53): Dendritic cell proximity to tumor, facilitating antigen presentation and T cell priming.
- CD4T–Macrophages (z = −6.5, HR = 0.33, 95% CI: 0.22–0.50): CD4+ T helper cells coordinating with macrophages, supporting adaptive immune responses.

All four motifs exhibit strong negative z-scores (indicating significant spatial enrichment in long survivors) and low hazard ratios (HR = 0.28–0.35), confirming their protective association with patient outcomes. These findings align with established immunology: immune cell infiltration into tumor cores correlates with improved prognosis across multiple cancer types.

b, Risk score distribution. Histogram showing the distribution of model-predicted risk scores across the patient cohort. Risk scores range from 0.50 to 0.70, with a relatively uniform distribution indicating the model discriminates across the full risk spectrum rather than clustering predictions at extremes. The spread of scores enables meaningful patient stratification for clinical decision-making.

c, Attention-risk correlation. Scatter plot examining the relationship between mean attention weight (x-axis) and predicted risk score (y-axis) at the patient level. A weak negative correlation is observed (Pearson r = −0.234, P = 0.018), suggesting that patients with higher average attention weights on specific tissue regions tend to have marginally lower predicted risk. 

This inverse relationship reflects that the model allocates attention broadly across immune-infiltrated (prognostically favorable) regions, whereas high-risk cases often show focal attention on specific tumor niches. Note: This does not contradict Fig. 4a, which shows that within a slide, high-attention regions contribute to the final risk score; here we examine between-patient variation in mean attention, which captures a different aspect of model behavior. The modest correlation strength reflects the complex, non-linear nature of the attention-to-outcome relationship, where spatial patterns matter more than aggregate attention intensity.

Abbreviations: HR, hazard ratio; CD8T, CD8+ T cells; CD4T, CD4+ T cells; DC, dendritic cells; Macr, macrophages; Tumor, tumor cells.
