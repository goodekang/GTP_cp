Supplementary Figure S6 | Detailed training curves and convergence analysis.

a, Loss curves during training. Training loss (teal) and validation loss (orange) plotted over 200 epochs. Both curves exhibit smooth convergence, with training loss decreasing from ~1.0 to ~0.2 and validation loss stabilizing at ~0.3. The consistent gap between training and validation loss (generalization gap) remains moderate throughout training, indicating effective regularization. Light shaded regions represent variance across training runs.

b, C-index curves during training. Training C-index (teal) and validation C-index (orange) plotted over 200 epochs on the BRCA cohort (single cancer type). Training C-index increases from ~0.55 to ~0.90, while validation C-index rises from ~0.55 and plateaus at ~0.78. The best validation performance (C-index = 0.780) is achieved at epoch 99, as indicated by the annotation. This single-cohort validation C-index is consistent with the BRCA-specific value in Supplementary Fig. S3 (0.73) within expected variance; differences from pan-cancer averages (Fig. 3a: 0.786) reflect cohort-specific characteristics. Beyond epoch 99, training C-index continues to improve while validation C-index stagnates, suggesting the onset of overfitting. This epoch was selected for early stopping in final model selection.

c, Learning rate schedule. Visualization of the cosine annealing learning rate schedule (purple filled area) over 200 epochs. The learning rate starts at 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ and smoothly decays following a cosine function, approaching zero by epoch 200. Cosine annealing provides gradual learning rate reduction without abrupt transitions, enabling fine-grained optimization in later epochs while avoiding local minima trapping. This schedule has been shown to improve convergence stability and final model performance compared to step decay schedules.

d, Train-validation gap analysis (overfitting monitor). Plot showing the difference between training and validation C-index (Train − Val Gap) over epochs. The teal line tracks this generalization gap, which starts near zero (indicating matched performance) and gradually increases to ~0.10–0.12 by epoch 200. 

The pink shaded region indicates the "Overfitting risk" zone where the gap exceeds acceptable thresholds. The horizontal dashed line at 0.00 represents perfect generalization (no train-val gap). The gap remains in the acceptable range (teal region, "C-index gap") during early training but enters the overfitting risk zone after epoch ~75–100, providing visual justification for early stopping at epoch 99. This monitoring approach enables data-driven selection of training duration to maximize generalization performance.

Training configuration: AdamW optimizer, initial learning rate 1 × 10⁻⁴, weight decay 0.01, batch size 32, cosine annealing schedule with 200 epochs maximum, early stopping patience 20 epochs based on validation C-index.

Abbreviations: C-index, concordance index; Val, validation.
